## Wait, what?

Yes. Some peons started playing cards in Azeroth, and now... well, look at this. It's everywhere.

I'm not kidding. Of course you won't get a 1:1 Hearthstone experience here, but a slightly reduced one: simpler animations and no special sound effects. But you can play the game. I recreated the original, vanilla Hearthstone. No crazy expansions, just the original game that I, at least, kind of miss.

## What is included?

Well, go out, play, and find out for yourself.

You can find Ezra Chatterton in Orgrimmar. He teaches everyone who wants to learn the game. The same goes for Ibelin Redmoore in Stormwind. They hang out in the lesser-known inns, so you might have to ask around, but I'm sure someone in each city can lead the way.

After a short introduction, you can play as much as you want. You just need to unlock the classes first, but again: go out, play, and you will find someone who helps you with that.

## Background
I feel like I'm not the only one who misses the old days of Hearthstone, am I? When the game was slower and more focused on control. I can't quite explain it, but the old versions felt cooler to me. Less over the top. I missed that, so I thought: why not port it over?

I started about a year ago for my Azerothcore fork. I never fully finished that , but with the help of AI I managed to port it over to Windrunner and Lua 5, and this time I actually finished it. You will find a lot of quests connected to Hearthstone and the world around it. You get coins for playing, whether you win or lose, and I'm not forcing you to play it. It's like every other game that has a card game built in, like Gwent in The Witcher, or the Final Fantasy one, I enjoyed that a lot back then.

WoW already has a lot of content, but especially in early versions like vanilla there are no pet battles, and you can't farm transmogs like you can in retail. I feel like something is missing: content you can just do without thinking. Well, Hearthstone might be the wrong game for "without thinking", you do need a brain cell or two to play it. But it's cool side content that you never have to touch, and if you want to, you can spend a lot of hours collecting cards and playing matches. I don't even want to know how many hours I spent just testing the things I added and hunting for bugs.

And I'm still finding them, don't get me wrong. This is not a perfect. Most of the card effects are unique enough that you can't simply copy them over. I think my framework is solid, but some of the cards are a bit more complicated than others. You'll see what I mean.

I just hope you enjoy what I created here. And collect a few cards for me.

This whole thing arrives with version 1.19.0, along with a few other changes. So stay tuned, it will be out in the next weeks. I also updated the roadmap again.
