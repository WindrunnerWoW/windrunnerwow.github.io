Well met!

## Version Update.

Today is the time for release 1.18.3. You can find all the release notes [here](/changelog/1-18-3).

If you use the Launcher, it should ask you to update. If you don't, may god be with you.

## In short

A new system was introduced: the companion guild. Read more about that [here](/news/FS_companionGuild) and [here](/companion-guild). Instead of having bots run around the world and do nothing while hogging system resources, breaking immersion and being stupid in general, you now recruit bots in inn's. You wanna do a hard quest and need help? Look for an inn. You wanna run a dungeon? Inn!

Also, a lot of improvements to the bots and dungeon clear. The newest patch comes with a working Dungeon Clear Addon, the bots don't break in dungeons anymore, bosses drop money (DC was somehow blocking that) and much, muuuuuch more.

Raids are now also scaled down to 20 players. No more 40 player raids, especially with bots this is aweful. This could still cause issues, so please, feel free to hit me up if anything doesn't work anymore.
