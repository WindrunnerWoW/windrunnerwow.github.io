### What is the Material Storage

The materila storage is a special bank, that allows you to store a LOT of materials. Each one has a designated spot, and you can store... hopefully more than you will ever need.

### How to use it

First you need to buy it. To do that you visit a banker and you pay 250g. Then the material storage option at bankers will open it.

### But Master, please tell me more

Sure, sure. 

As i already talked about in the [Spotlight for Crafting Orders](/news/FS_craftingStorage), people are playing to have fun. Of course, hauling 1000kg (2204.62lbs for the yankees) of linen, because you need to get yourself some new crafting gear, is no fun. And it taking away all your bank space is also no fun. So instead, you can bank it in your special material storage.

<br>

## Background

Fellow engineers,

all of us know the struggle. You pick a profession and it basically wants everything. Leather, cloth, a bit of ore here and there, and of course gems, because your bags were getting comfortable and that cannot be allowed. So where do you put all of that, especially while leveling, when half your inventory is already quest items ?

Ah. I see. You sell it, and you buy it back later, when you actually need it. Very nice... Material Storage does not solve that problem. It is expensive. So expensive that, unless you have been quietly mugging the auction house since level 10, you will not have the gold until you are somewhere around level 60 and have finally stopped spending every copper on training.

But if you are a hoarder like me, and selling a stack of linen feels like a personal failure, this is for you. You hit Deposit All, and every material you have ever picked up gets filed into its own little slot. No more "I know I had that copper bar. It is on a character. In a bag. Probably."

I built this right after Crafting Orders, looked at the pile of materials that feature assumes you own, and thought: where the hell do I store all of this. One change on the server is that gathering is secondary now, so you can take mining, herbalism and skinning together. You kind of have to, otherwise Crafting Orders is just a trainer asking for twenty things you cannot get. The part I maybe should have seen coming is that your bags and your bank then fill up like you are moving house every time you step outside.

There is already a storage module out there for Azerothcore, and it works through gossip. If you like that, fine, enjoy yourself. I don't. Whenever I make something for this server I try to make it feel like Blizzard could have shipped it in vanilla. I doubt they would have put a bank inside a gossip option.

The whole thing is basically Guild Wars 2's material storage. Theirs lives inside the bank. Mine opens its own window. I tried but couldnt make it work.

I also thought about letting crafting pull straight from the storage, so you never have to take anything out. Real quality of life. It also means your tailor is rummaging through a warehouse you are not standing in, which to me starts to feel like we left vanilla and wandered into a convenience store. If you feel differently, let me know in the discussion. I am not married to the extra clicking. I am just suspicious of convenience that breaks the bit.

Until then, farm some gold. The extra warehouse is only 250g. A bargain I guess?
