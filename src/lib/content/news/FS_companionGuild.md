### What is the Companion Guild

The Companion Guild is there for when the road gets dangerous. Out in the world you will run into elite foes, group quests, and dungeons you simply cannot clear alone. This is where the guild comes in: its battle-hardened adventurers will fight at your side — for a price.

You pay upfront for their time, and in return they share the risk — and the loot. They will never abandon you inside a dungeon, but if their hired time runs out while you are out in the open world, they move on.

### Where can I find these recruiters

A **recruiter** waits in each inn across Azeroth, so hiring help never means a long trip.

Browse who is available, choose a companion, and send them out with you. You can hire a single companion by class and specialization, or just hit Fill Party and let the guild top you up to one tank, one healer and three damage dealers. In a raid group that turns into Fill Raid for your 10, 20 or 40 man.

The guild handles the introduction. You handle the adventure.

### Temporary or permanent?
**Temporary** companions are short lived friends. You pay for the trip, they stay for a few hours. The stronger you get, the stronger — and pricier — they get, but no immediately. To get stronger companions, you need to hire new ones.

![Temporary Companions](/art/news/companion_recruiter_1.webp)

**Permanent** companions are yours to keep. You pay a high price once, they move into your personal roster, and from then on you can invite them back whenever you want, for free.

They never fall behind, either. Even while they wait for your call, they keep training — every time you summon them they return at your level, with fresh gear to match.

## Background

Hello hello and welcome back,

So maybe I'm the only one who thinks like this (and when seeing other projects for "offline singleplayer servers" this really seems to be the case), but I find it kind of immersion-breaking to see all these headless bots running around the world. A lot of people crank that up to the max and have 20 active bots in the zone while 9,000 of them are AFK somewhere else and only activate as you get near them. I don't like that. I feel like it's kind of weird to have bots running around pseudo-questing, if you want to call it that. I find it way more immersive to be the only one out there doing the things the people in towns and villages ask for.

Why would I be the 700th person killing six Murlocs? Why would that small tauren camp need 10,000 livers a day to survive? I find that kind of weird, so I instead opted to go with something I like better.

I don't even know right now from which game exactly I copied this. I think somewhere you can hire short-lived helpers... But I definitely saw it somewhere. This is not an XCOM thing, but it's kind of similar. You of course don't necessarily have them forever, and if they die they are not gone. But you hire someone to help you, which I think in this world is quite fitting: instead of having thousands of people running around everywhere, YOU help people in need, who have noone else who helps them, and you are not the sixth person today to bring back a medallion. In return, YOU rely on the help of others. Other adventurers out there that want to make a living doing stuff. So in this case, you are kind of the quest giver for the short time they're with you.

Now I created this for AzerothCore where there are, in my opinion, way better playerbots. But after a lot of adjustments, I also made it kind of work here. Expect some difficulties. But yeah, I will keep working on them to get the best version of the playerbots so we can keep the game immersive and make it feel like a singleplayer game. So no weird scaling to solo dungeon, but you just hire four bots, pay a little of your money, and they help you.

I hope I am not alone in this. If you feel differently and want bots running around, you can disable the *WindrunnerCompanionMode* in the config and play this other version. But please don't expect any support if you do that. Cause I am actively working against that with a lot of changes I made.

This feature will go live soon, in patch 1.18.3.

Stay tuned and have a nice time!
